# Product

## Register

product

## Users

EuroLeague fans, primarily playing casually on phones. They want to start a
quiz quickly, understand the rules at a glance, and challenge themselves or
other fans without reading a marketing-style introduction first.

## Product Purpose

EuroLeague Quiz turns basketball knowledge into fast, replayable games across
players, clubs, seasons, careers, photos, and statistics. Success means a
visitor can choose a game and begin playing within seconds, with Tic-Tac-Toe as
the flagship entry point and the other modes easy to scan and launch.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, clear, and competitive. The experience should feel knowledgeable
and basketball-specific without becoming dense, loud, or arcade-like.

## Anti-references

- Text-heavy landing pages that explain the product before exposing a playable
  action.
- Oversized mobile typography that pushes the game launcher below the fold.
- Generic card dashboards where every game has equal visual weight and no
  obvious starting point.
- Decorative motion that delays frequent actions or makes game information
  harder to read.

## Design Principles

1. Put play before explanation on every viewport.
2. Make the next action obvious within the first viewport.
3. Preserve one clear hierarchy: flagship game first, alternatives easy to
   scan.
4. Use basketball identity and real content as the personality, not ornamental
   UI.
5. Keep interaction feedback immediate and motion purposeful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, readable type at phone sizes,
generous touch targets, keyboard and screen-reader semantics, and
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every non-essential movement.
